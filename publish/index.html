Situations

When to bring us in

  • You have to decide where to invest in technology and security, and in what order.
  • You are preparing for an ENS, ISO 27001 or PCI DSS audit.
  • You need a CISO but not a permanent appointment.
  • You are about to buy, sell or invest in a company.
  • You are moving systems to the cloud or overhauling identity and access management.
  • You need to check how far an attack would get.
  • You want to know what an attacker can see from the outside.

How we work

We start with what your organisation sets out to achieve.

From there we agree priorities and investment with senior management, design the architecture and see it through.

We choose the technology to suit your environment and the team that will live with it.

Every offensive test begins with written authorisation from the owner of the systems and a scope agreed in advance.

Services

From security governance to threat intelligence

In these areas we advise senior management and work with your technology and security teams.

  1. 01Governance & compliance

    Govern security like the rest of the business

    We define who decides and who is accountable for security, translate technical risk into business terms and prepare your organisation for ENS, ISO 27001, PCI DSS and GDPR audits. If you do not have a CISO, we take on the role as a service.

  2. 02Corporate transactions

    Know the technology risk before you sign

    We carry out technology and cybersecurity due diligence for buyers, sellers and investors, with findings you can take into the negotiation. Once the deal has closed, we advise on the integration.

  3. 03Architecture & technology

    Security architecture designed around your plans

    We design, implement and run Zero Trust architectures: identity and privileged access (IAM and PAM), network segmentation, secure access (SASE and ZTNA), detection and response (EDR and XDR), cloud security posture and data protection (DLP). We also supply the technology.

  4. 04Offensive security

    Test your defences with authorised attacks

    We run penetration tests on web and mobile applications, APIs, infrastructure and directory services, cloud environments and industrial (OT) systems, as well as red team exercises and social engineering.

  5. 05Threat intelligence

    See beyond your perimeter

    We continuously monitor your organisation’s external exposure and the threats aimed at your sector, including on the dark web, and alert you to what needs attention.
