This policy explains how SlapSec LLC (“SlapSec”, “we”, “us”) handles personal data collected through slapsec.com. We collect only what we need to reply to you and to run the site.

01 Data controller

Controller
SlapSec LLC, 412 W 7th St, Clovis, NM 88101, USA
Privacy contact
info@slapsec.com

EU, EEA and UK visitors

Because we offer services to clients in the European Union, the EU GDPR and the UK GDPR may apply to your personal data. The sections below set out the legal bases we rely on and the rights you can exercise. As a controller based in the United States, we handle these requests at info@slapsec.com.

02 What we collect

Information you give us

When you email us, we receive the details you choose to include, such as your name, your organisation, your contact details and the content of your message.

Technical data

When you visit the site, our hosting and content delivery provider automatically processes standard technical information, such as your IP address, browser type, the pages requested and the time of each request, to deliver and secure the site. The country a request comes from, derived from its IP address, is used at that moment to show visitors browsing from Spain the Spanish version first; we do not keep it.

A functional cookie

If you choose English on the Spanish version, a functional cookie remembers that choice; our cookie policy describes it. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, we do not run web analytics, and the site loads nothing from third parties.

03 Purposes and legal bases

  • To reply to your enquiry and to take the steps you ask for before a possible engagement: pre-contractual steps or the performance of a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)) and, where applicable, your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)).
  • To run and secure the site: our legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • To comply with legal obligations, where they apply (Art. 6(1)(c)).

For visitors in the United States, we process this data in accordance with applicable US law.

04 Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with the service providers that help us run the site and our email, acting on our instructions as processors:

  • Netlify, Inc., for hosting and content delivery;
  • our email provider, to receive and reply to your messages.

We may also disclose data where the law requires it or to protect our rights.

05 International transfers

We are based in the United States. If you are in the EEA or the UK, your personal data is transferred to and processed in the United States. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for these transfers, such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses. By contacting us, you understand that your data will be processed in the United States.

06 How long we keep it

We keep enquiry data only for as long as we need it to handle your request and any business relationship that follows, plus any period the law requires. After that, we delete or anonymise it.

07 Your rights

EEA and UK (GDPR)

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and you may withdraw your consent at any time. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

California (CCPA and CPRA)

You have the right to know, access, delete and correct your personal information, and to opt out of its “sale” or “sharing”. We do not sell or share your personal information, and we will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

To exercise any of these rights, write to info@slapsec.com. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

08 Security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, and the site is served only over HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information appropriately.

09 Children

This site is intended for professional users and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10 Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the version in force. Questions or requests: info@slapsec.com.
